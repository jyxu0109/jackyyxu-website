Teaching Philosophy

Understanding before memorization.

Great teaching is not only about telling students what to play. It is about helping them understand why.

Jacky Y. Xu’s teaching emphasizes musical understanding, technical control, confidence, discipline, and the ability to solve problems independently.

Rather than relying only on repetition or correction, students are encouraged to recognize the causes behind mistakes, evaluate their own playing, and make informed musical decisions.

The goal is not simply to prepare for one examination, audition, or performance. It is to develop the skills and self-awareness needed for continued growth beyond the classroom.

Student Development

Students remember how they learned, not only what they learned.

Every student brings a different combination of experience, motivation, learning style, and goals.

Jacky adapts instruction to the individual while maintaining clear expectations for preparation, responsibility, musical understanding, and long-term development.

Students are encouraged to become active participants in the learning process rather than passive recipients of correction.

Teaching Beyond the Lesson

Extending meaningful feedback beyond the classroom.

Jacky is developing an AI-powered percussion practice assistant designed to help musicians receive useful, musician-centered feedback between lessons.

The project explores how technology can support rhythm recognition, timing analysis, targeted practice, and greater independence without replacing the role of a thoughtful teacher.
