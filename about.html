About

A percussionist, educator, and product builder based in New York.

Stern Auditorium / Perelman Stage
Carnegie Hall · New York

Jacky Y. Xu works across performance, education, production, and technology.

Rooted in classical percussion, his work extends beyond the concert stage into teaching, product development, and the broader systems that support professional music-making.

Jacky earned both a Bachelor of Music in Percussion Performance and a Master of Music in Percussion Performance from New York University.

His experience includes collaborations with internationally recognized artists such as Lang Lang and Joe Hisaishi, alongside work connected with Carnegie Hall, Lincoln Center, Aspen Music Festival and School, American Composers Orchestra, American Symphony Orchestra, and other professional arts organizations.

Across each area of his practice, Jacky brings the same priorities: artistic integrity, thoughtful problem-solving, and the belief that music can create meaningful experiences both on and beyond the stage.
